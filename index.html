📖 Kadha.in

Manassine thottu pokunna kathakal

❤️ Oru Cheriya Pranayam

Arun enna oru payyan oru kochu gramathil jeevichirunnu. Avante jeevitham valare sadharanamayirunnu. Oru divasam bus stop-il vech avan Meera enna penkuttiye kandumutti.

Aadyam avar randuperum aparichitharayirunnu. Pinneed cheriya samsarangaliloode avar nalla suhruthukkal ayi. Avarude friendship divasam kazhiyumbol kooduthal shakthamayi.

Jeevithathil pala prashnangal vannalum avar parasparam support cheythu. Sathyamaya sneham ennath valiya karyangal alla, oru aalude santhoshathinum vishamathinum koode nilkkunnathaanu.

Angane oru cheriya parichayam avarude jeevithathile ettavum manoharamaya ormayayi maari.

🌧️ Mazhayile Ormakal

Rahul oru divasam mazha peyyunna vaikunneram pazhaya vazhiyiloode nadannu. Aa vazhiyil avante pazhaya ormakal ellam thirichu vannu.

Kure varshangalkku munpu aa sthalath vech aanu avan oru nalla suhruthine kandath. Aa friendship avante jeevithathile vilappetta oru bhagamayi maari.

Samayam munnott poyalum chila ormakal nammade manassil ennum nilanilkkum.

✨ Swapnangalude Vazhi

Vishnu enna oru payyan valiya swapnangal undayirunnu. Pakshe avante vazhiyil orupadu thadassangal undayi.

Avan thalarathe parishramichu. Oro divasavum avan thante lakshyathilekku oru cheriya chuvad vechu.

Oduvil avante kashtappadukalkku phalam kitti. Swapnangal kaanan mathram alla, athinu vendi parishramikkanum venam enn avan manasilakki.
